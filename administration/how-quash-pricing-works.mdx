Quash uses a custom monthly or annual agreement around your testing workload: runs, apps, people, devices and parallel capacity. There is no self-serve checkout or universal public price. Terms are agreed with your team.

## Start on your own workflow

1. **Book a demo.** Walk the Quash team through your apps and the flows you want to test.
2. **Free guided trial.** After the demo, Quash configures your workspace, users, agreed devices and initial Runbooks for a free guided trial, typically 3–7 days. Onboarding and hands-on guidance help the team get started.
3. **Subscription or paid POC.** After the trial, choose a subscription, or discuss an optional paid month-long POC to validate more use cases and expected usage.

Dates, scope and commercial terms are agreed for your team.

## Understand the quote

Your proposal sets out:

* Run volume
* App and user scope
* Included device allocation
* Parallel capacity
* AI usage terms
* Any additional infrastructure or services

A proposal is specific to one agreement. It is not a public rate card, and its allowances and support terms do not carry over to other agreements.

## What counts as a run

* **A run is usually one test case.**
* **A Runbook can consume several runs.** A Runbook can contain several test cases.
* **Failed and cancelled runs count.** AI-driven device executions count even when they fail or are cancelled.
* **The surface does not matter.** Runs started from a compatible MCP client count under the same usage terms as runs started from the web interface.

For anything else, use the usage terms agreed for your setup.

## Devices and parallel capacity

Device allocation and parallel capacity are scoped per subscription, and the Quash device fleet grows with demand. Supported targets include Android physical devices and emulators, physical iPhones and iOS simulators, Quash cloud Android and iOS devices, and browsers.

See [Devices](/devices/overview) for setup.

## AI usage and model keys

Your quote states the AI usage terms for your agreement. Using your own model provider keys is scoped for larger enterprise arrangements.

## Other requirements

If you need a private deployment, specific identity controls, integrations, or support terms, raise them during the demo or trial. Quash assesses and scopes these requirements with your team, and what is included is set out in your agreement.

## Common questions

**Is there a free plan?**

No. Quash does not offer a free plan or self-serve checkout. After a demo, the initial guided trial is free and typically lasts 3–7 days.

**Is the POC free?**

No. The optional month-long POC is paid and scoped with your team. It comes after the free trial and is separate from it.

**Does a failed run count?**

Yes. AI-driven device executions count even when they fail or are cancelled.

**How does a Runbook count?**

A Runbook can contain several test cases, so one Runbook can consume several runs.

**Can I use the Quash MCP server?**

Yes. Compatible MCP clients have the same Quash features and device coverage as the web interface. See [Quash MCP](/quash-mcp/overview).

**Can I bring my own LLM API keys?**

Customer-owned model keys are scoped for larger enterprise arrangements. Raise it during your demo or trial.
