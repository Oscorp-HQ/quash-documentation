## Free trial

When you sign up, you get a 14-day free trial with 100 test minutes — no credit card required. The trial gives you full platform access so you can run real tasks, generate tests, and see how Quash works end to end.

The free trial does not include overage. Once the 100 minutes are used, you upgrade to a paid plan to continue.

## What counts as a test minute

A test minute is one minute of active test execution on a device — local or cloud. The timer runs while Mahoraga is executing instructions on the device. Time spent writing prompts, generating tests in Test Studio, or reviewing reports does not count.

## Overage

Included minutes cover normal team usage. When you exceed your plan's monthly allocation, Quash bills per-minute so short runs stay cheap and long suites stay predictable.

**Test minutes overage:** $0.20/min

Annual customers get 20% off overage rates. The free trial does not include overage.

## Common questions

**What counts as a "test minute"?**

A test minute is one minute of active test execution on a device — local or cloud. The timer runs while Mahoraga is executing instructions on the device. Time spent writing prompts, generating tests in Test Studio, or reviewing reports does not count.

**What if I exceed my plan's included minutes?**

You are billed at $0.20 per additional minute. Annual customers get 20% off overage rates. Usage is tracked in real time so there are no surprises.

**Can I add more apps?**

The number of apps under test is set by your plan. If you need more, upgrade to the next tier or contact us for Enterprise.

**What is the difference between cloud devices and the real device fleet?**

Cloud devices are shared, Quash-hosted Android devices available on all plans. The real device fleet is a set of dedicated devices assigned exclusively to your organisation — available as an add-on on Pro and included in Enterprise.

**How does annual billing work?**

Annual plans are billed yearly and save 20% on the monthly rate. Annual customers also get 20% off overage rates.

**How does the free trial work?**

Sign up and get 14 days with 100 test minutes, full platform access, no credit card required. When the trial ends or minutes run out, pick a plan to continue.

**Can I bring my own LLM API keys?**

Yes, on Enterprise. You can connect your own Azure OpenAI or Amazon Bedrock accounts so Quash runs on your preferred models.

**What is in Enterprise that I cannot get in Pro?**

Dedicated real device fleet (not an add-on), bring your own LLM API keys, test generation from code and Figma, SSO (SAML/OIDC), SCIM provisioning, on-prem / self-hosted deployment, private VPC, custom data residency, compliance documentation (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, DPDPA), implementation services, a dedicated CSM, custom SLA, and a full automation guarantee.

**I am a fintech or regulated company — what plan should I use?**

Enterprise. It includes SSO, SCIM, on-prem deployment, private VPC, custom data residency, and compliance documentation (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, DPDPA).

**I am already on a custom plan — what happens?**

Your existing plan is honoured. The Quash team will reach out to discuss how the new tiers map to your current arrangement and whether any changes apply. Nothing changes automatically — you will be contacted before any migration.

For more details, visit the [pricing page on the Quash website](https://quashbugs.com/pricing).
