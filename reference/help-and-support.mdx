#### Security & Privacy <a href="#security-and-privacy" id="security-and-privacy"></a>

Quash prioritizes data protection and privacy:

* All device communications are **encrypted end-to-end**.
* Task recordings are stored securely in your **S3 bucket**.
* No sensitive data is sent to third-party services.
* Local device access only – no remote control features.
* **Privacy Note:** Quash processes all automation locally on your machine. LLM requests are sent only to your configured AI provider with **task descriptions** — no personal data or screenshots are shared.
