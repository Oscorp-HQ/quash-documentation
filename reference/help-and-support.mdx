#### Security & Privacy <a href="#security-and-privacy" id="security-and-privacy"></a>

How Quash handles your testing data:

* **Use test environments.** Run Quash against UAT or test builds, with test accounts and suitable test data.
* **AI processing.** Relevant data is sent to AI models to execute your tests. It is not used for training.
* **Local devices.** Testing on your own devices does not mean data stays off AI models. Relevant data is still sent to AI models during execution.
* **Retention.** Quash retains data for 90 days by default. Longer retention is available by arrangement. Quash also keeps backups.
* **Private deployment.** If you have private deployment requirements, Quash can assess and scope them with your team.

For questions about model providers, regions, credential protection, or deletion, contact the Quash team.
