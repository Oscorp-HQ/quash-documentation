---
title: "iOS Simulators (Mac only)"
description: "Run Quash tests on the iOS Simulator from your Mac using Xcode and the Devices panel."
---

1. Open **Xcode** on your Mac
2. Launch an iOS simulator from **Xcode → Open Developer Tool → Simulator**
3. In the Devices section on the Quash app, click **"Scan for devices"**. Your simulator should appear in the list.
4. Click **"Connect"** next to the simulator

    Your iOS simulator will now appear as an option in the prompt box when you click **Devices.**

    > ⚠️ **iOS notes:**
    >
    > * iOS simulators are **only available on Mac**
    > * **Physical iPhones** are supported. See [Physical iOS Devices](/devices/physical-ios-devices)
    > * **Quash cloud devices** include iOS devices. See [Cloud Device](/devices/cloud-device)
